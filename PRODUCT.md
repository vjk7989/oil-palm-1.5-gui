# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PalmWatch is used by system administrators, executive leaders, plantation heads, area managers, and field staff monitoring oil-palm operations in Andhra Pradesh. Each role needs to move from a portfolio view to its permitted districts, mandals, villages, farms, and trees without seeing records outside its assignment.

## Product Purpose

Provide a dependable AP-only operational explorer for reviewing planted area, palm counts, survey coverage, suspected health issues, pending work, farm layouts, and individual tree history. Success means users can trust that totals reconcile with the visible demo records, geographic markers correspond to real public places, and RBAC never broadens their scope.

## Brand Personality

Operational, approachable, and precise. The experience keeps the compact pink/rose visual language of the approved Buckleson reference while presenting agricultural evidence calmly and clearly.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic national dashboard, an abstract map with arbitrary marker placement, or a marketing page that mixes public Godrej figures with role-scoped operational totals. Do not imply that public place names or simulated farm coordinates establish ownership.

## Design Principles

- Geography is an interface: every map marker, table row, breadcrumb, and drill-down selection represents the same scoped record.
- Reconcile before presenting: visible totals are derived from the same farm records shown to the user.
- Evidence has provenance: official public context, public geography, and deterministic demo operations are labelled separately.
- Scope only narrows: role changes, resets, and direct navigation never reveal a broader assignment.
- Make status legible without relying on color alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All map data has a keyboard-accessible table/list equivalent, focus is visible, health states use text and color, and responsive layouts support desktop, tablet, and mobile use with reduced-motion preferences respected.
